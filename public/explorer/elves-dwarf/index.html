Browsing the satellite systems

Pick a host from the dropdown (or the list on the left) and the view jumps to it, framed on its virial radius. Every satellite candidate around that host is drawn as a circle, coloured by spectroscopic/SBF status. Click a circle — or a name in the list — to fly to that satellite and see its measurements and a Legacy Surveys cutout.

The status chips in the top bar are also filters — click one to show or hide that class. Rejected candidates (confirmed background objects) start hidden; click the chip to bring them in.

Use the View center fields to read the live center of the image or move to another position. Decimal degrees and sexagesimal coordinates are accepted.

Imaging comes from CDS. Legacy Surveys covers most of the sample but thins out below dec ≈ −30°; for the southern hosts use DSS or GALEX from the survey dropdown, or Aladin's own layer control at the bottom right.
